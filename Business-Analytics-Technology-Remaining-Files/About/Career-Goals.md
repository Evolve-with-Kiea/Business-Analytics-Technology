# Career Goals

## Approach

I have not selected one permanent career path. My goal is to explore how business analytics, data analytics, business intelligence, finance, accounting, marketing, and operations overlap, and to let coursework, practice, and projects show where my interests and strengths fit best. The roles I am considering are described in [Roles to Explore](../Career-Interests/Roles-to-Explore.md).

These goals are directional rather than deadline-driven. I will adjust them as I learn.

## Guiding Goals

1. **Understand business first.** Build working knowledge of how businesses earn revenue, control costs, and measure performance.
2. **Build practical technical skills.** Develop Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI skills through practice tied to business questions.
3. **Document evidence.** Keep a record of what I have learned and built so my skills can be verified.
4. **Communicate analysis clearly.** Learn to turn data into findings and recommendations that a non-technical audience can use.
5. **Stay open to direction.** Use exploration to narrow my options without committing too early.
