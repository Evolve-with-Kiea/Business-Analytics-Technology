# About Me

This page describes how I approach my professional development. It is a living document that I update as my education, skills, and projects progress.

## Overview

I am building a professional foundation at the intersection of business and technology. My focus is on understanding how businesses operate, how their performance is measured, how data is interpreted, and how analytical tools help organizations make better decisions.

I do not want to learn software in isolation. For every tool I study, I want to understand the business problem it solves and the decision it supports.

## Areas of Interest

- Business Analytics and Data Analytics
- Business Intelligence and Data Visualization
- Financial Analysis, Accounting, and Financial Reporting
- Marketing Analytics and E-commerce Analytics
- Business Strategy and Business Technology
- Business Process Improvement and Operational Analysis

## Current Focus

- Building a foundation in business concepts, accounting, and finance.
- Learning Microsoft Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI in a deliberate sequence.
- Learning Python through freeCodeCamp, with an emphasis on understanding concepts rather than copying code.
- Preparing for planned January 2027 coursework in BAS 120 and BAS 150.

## How I Learn

1. **Concept first.** I learn what a metric, formula, or technique means before I use a tool to calculate it.
2. **Business context.** I connect each skill to a realistic business question.
3. **Practice and documentation.** I record what I practice in the [Practice Log](../Learning-Roadmap/Practice-Log.md) and explain concepts in my own words.
4. **Evidence over claims.** I only describe a skill as developed when I can point to work that shows it. See [Skills Evidence](../Skills/Skills-Evidence.md).

## Business Interests

I am interested in applying analytics to real business scenarios, including e-commerce, digital products, marketing, and business performance measurement. These are areas I want to explore and apply over time. They are not claims of completed analytical projects. See [Specialization Areas](../Career-Interests/Specialization-Areas.md).
