# Professional Statement

## Purpose

This document holds the statements I use to describe my professional direction. Each version is intentionally limited to what is true today. I revise them as my education, skills, and projects grow.

## Core Statement

I am developing a professional foundation in business analytics and technology. My goal is to understand how businesses operate and how performance is measured, and to use analytical tools such as Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI to support better decisions. I am building both business knowledge and technical capability so that the tools I learn are tied to real business problems.

## Short Versions

**One sentence**

I am building skills in business analytics and technology to help organizations understand their performance and make better decisions.

**Two sentences**

I study the connection between business knowledge, analytical thinking, and technology. I am developing practical skills in Excel, Python, SQL, and Power BI, guided by the business questions each tool can answer.

## Positioning

My professional positioning rests on three connected ideas:

| Idea | What it means for how I work |
|---|---|
| Business knowledge | I learn accounting, finance, marketing, and operations concepts so I can interpret numbers in context. |
| Analytical thinking | I define the question, examine the data, test assumptions, and state limitations. |
| Technology | I use tools to make analysis accurate, repeatable, and easy to communicate. |

## Guidelines for Updating This Statement

- Add a credential only after I have earned it, and record it in [Certifications Earned](../Certifications/Certifications-Earned.md).
- Add a skill claim only when it is supported by an entry in [Skills Evidence](../Skills/Skills-Evidence.md).
- Add a measurable accomplishment only when I can document how it was measured.
- Keep language specific. Avoid vague claims such as "expert" or "results-driven."
