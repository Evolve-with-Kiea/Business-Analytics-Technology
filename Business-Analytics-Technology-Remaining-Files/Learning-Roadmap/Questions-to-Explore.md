# Questions to Explore

These questions guide my reading, practice, and project ideas. I mark a question as explored when I can answer it in writing and link to supporting work.

## Business Fundamentals

- [ ] How do revenue, profit, and cash flow differ, and why can a profitable business still run out of cash?
- [ ] How do the three financial statements connect to each other?
- [ ] What separates a useful KPI from a vanity metric?
- [ ] How do fixed and variable costs affect pricing and break-even decisions?

## Excel

- [ ] When is a spreadsheet the right tool, and when does it become a risk?
- [ ] How can I structure a workbook so others can audit it?
- [ ] What makes a dashboard useful rather than cluttered?

## Python

- [ ] How do I decide whether to solve a problem in Excel or Python?
- [ ] Which repetitive business tasks are good candidates for automation?
- [ ] What should I understand about data types before using data libraries?

## SQL and Databases

- [ ] How do relationships between tables prevent duplicate or inconsistent data?
- [ ] What mistakes commonly produce incorrect JOIN results?
- [ ] How do I check that an aggregated result is correct?

## Power BI and Business Intelligence

- [ ] How does a good data model make DAX calculations easier?
- [ ] How should a report differ for executives and operational managers?
- [ ] How do I keep reports accurate as data refreshes?

## Marketing, Sales, and E-commerce

- [ ] Which measures best show whether marketing spending is effective?
- [ ] How do customer acquisition cost and customer lifetime value work together?
- [ ] What data would I need to understand customer behavior in an online store?

## Operations and Process Improvement

- [ ] How do I identify a bottleneck rather than a symptom?
- [ ] What is the difference between efficiency and effectiveness?
- [ ] How should I measure whether a process change worked?

## Statistics and Analytical Judgment

- [ ] When does correlation suggest a business action, and when does it mislead?
- [ ] How much data is enough to trust a result?
- [ ] How should I communicate uncertainty to a non-technical audience?

## Career

- [ ] Which roles combine business knowledge and technology in the way I enjoy most?
- [ ] Which skills appear most often in the job postings I research?
- [ ] Which certifications are valued in roles I am considering?
