# Learning Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap organizes my development into five phases. It is designed to be flexible.

## Principles

- **Flexible timing.** I do not assign fixed deadlines. Pace depends on coursework, work, and life commitments.
- **Overlap is expected.** I may work on Python while strengthening Excel, or begin SQL before finishing every Phase 2 topic.
- **Depth over speed.** Understanding a concept well is more valuable than finishing a checklist.
- **Business context throughout.** Each tool is tied to a business question.

## Phase Overview

| Phase | Theme | Main tools and subjects |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Business and Excel Foundations | Business concepts, accounting, finance, Excel |
| 2 | Programming and Analytical Practice | Python, programming logic, Excel dashboards, case studies |
| 3 | Databases and Business Intelligence | SQL, database fundamentals, Power BI, data modeling |
| 4 | Specialized Business Analytics | Financial, marketing, sales, and operational analytics; KPIs |
| 5 | Professional Application | Portfolio, projects, certifications, recommendations |

## Phase 1: Business and Excel Foundations

**Focus:** Build the business and spreadsheet base that later work depends on.

### Topics

- [ ] Business concepts: revenue, costs, profit, markets, and operations
- [ ] Accounting and financial fundamentals: the three financial statements and basic terminology
- [ ] Excel: formulas and functions
- [ ] Excel: data organization and cleaning
- [ ] Basic business analysis: summarizing, comparing, and charting data

### Practice Ideas

- Rebuild a simple income statement in Excel and explain each line
- Clean a sample dataset and document each cleaning decision
- Calculate a margin or growth rate and write one paragraph interpreting it

### Business Connection

Understanding how a business earns money and measures results gives meaning to every later analysis.

### Signs I Am Ready to Move Forward

- I can explain the main financial statements in plain language.
- I can organize and clean a dataset in Excel without help.
- I can write a short interpretation of a basic analysis.

## Phase 2: Programming and Analytical Practice

**Focus:** Learn programming logic and apply analytical methods to realistic scenarios.

### Topics

- [ ] Python fundamentals: variables, data types, strings, numbers
- [ ] Programming logic: conditionals, loops, and functions
- [ ] Python collections: lists and dictionaries
- [ ] Practical exercises that apply Python to simple business calculations
- [ ] Excel dashboards: PivotTables, charts, and KPI layouts
- [ ] Business case studies: framing a problem and recommending action

### Practice Ideas

- After each freeCodeCamp lesson, rewrite the concept in my own words
- Modify exercises instead of copying them, then explain the change
- Build a small Excel dashboard for a sample business scenario

### Business Connection

Programming and dashboards turn repeated calculations into repeatable processes and clear reporting.

### Signs I Am Ready to Move Forward

- I can write small Python programs and explain each line.
- I can build a basic Excel dashboard and justify its design.
- I can complete a short case study from problem to recommendation.

## Phase 3: Databases and Business Intelligence

**Focus:** Work with relational data and build interactive reports.

### Topics

- [ ] SQL: SELECT, filtering, sorting, aggregate functions, GROUP BY, and JOINs
- [ ] Database fundamentals: tables, keys, and relationships
- [ ] Power BI: importing and transforming data
- [ ] Power BI: data modeling and relationships
- [ ] Power BI: DAX fundamentals, visualizations, reports, and dashboards

### Practice Ideas

- Write queries that answer specific business questions
- Build a small data model with at least two related tables
- Create a report with a clear audience and purpose

### Business Connection

Most organizational data lives in databases, and business intelligence tools present it to decision-makers.

### Signs I Am Ready to Move Forward

- I can query related tables and explain the results.
- I can model data and create measures in Power BI.
- I can describe who a report is for and what decision it supports.

## Phase 4: Specialized Business Analytics

**Focus:** Apply skills to specific business functions and measurement.

### Topics

- [ ] Financial analytics
- [ ] Marketing analytics
- [ ] Sales analytics
- [ ] Operational analytics
- [ ] KPI development
- [ ] Profitability analysis
- [ ] Business performance measurement

### Practice Ideas

- Define a KPI set for a hypothetical business and explain each choice
- Analyze profitability by product or segment using sample data
- Compare marketing channels using standard performance measures

### Business Connection

Specialization shows how the same tools answer different questions for finance, marketing, sales, and operations.

### Signs I Am Ready to Move Forward

- I can select appropriate KPIs for a given business goal.
- I can interpret results and note their limitations.
- I have a clearer sense of which specialization interests me most.

## Phase 5: Professional Application

**Focus:** Turn learning into shareable evidence and professional readiness.

### Topics

- [ ] Portfolio development
- [ ] Practical business projects
- [ ] Certifications
- [ ] Analytical recommendations
- [ ] Process improvement
- [ ] Professional development: resume, LinkedIn, and networking

### Practice Ideas

- Publish two or three documented projects with data sources and limitations
- Pursue one or two certifications from the [Certification Plan](../Certifications/Certification-Plan.md)
- Update my resume and LinkedIn using only verified information

### Business Connection

Documented projects and credentials make my skills visible to employers, instructors, collaborators, and future clients.

### Signs I Am Ready to Move Forward

- My projects are documented well enough for someone else to follow.
- My resume and LinkedIn match my actual evidence.
- I can discuss my work and its limitations clearly.

## Reviewing the Roadmap

I review this roadmap after each course or major project and adjust the phases to match what I have learned. Record changes in my [Practice Log](Practice-Log.md).
