# Industry Research

## Purpose

This document is where I research roles, industries, employers, and trends. Its purpose is to base my career decisions on evidence and to keep my learning aligned with real expectations.

## Research Questions

- Which skills and tools appear most often in postings for roles I am exploring?
- Which certifications do employers mention?
- How do responsibilities differ between roles with similar titles?
- Which industries use analytics in ways that interest me?
- What do professionals say about entry points and growth?

## Research Method

1. Choose a role from [Roles to Explore](../Career-Interests/Roles-to-Explore.md).
2. Review several current job postings and note repeated requirements.
3. Check official sources such as the [Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) and [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org/).
4. Talk with professionals when possible.
5. Record findings below and compare them with my [Technical Skills](../Skills/Technical-Skills.md).
