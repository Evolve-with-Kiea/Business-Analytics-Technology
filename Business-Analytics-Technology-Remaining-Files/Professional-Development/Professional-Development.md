## Portfolio Development

A portfolio is a set of documented projects that others can review.

**Each project should include:**

- [ ] The business question
- [ ] The data source and any limitations
- [ ] Methods and tools used
- [ ] Findings, written clearly
- [ ] Recommendations
- [ ] Assumptions and limitations

**Selection principles**

- Prefer fewer, well-documented projects over many unfinished ones.
- Use public, synthetic, or properly permitted data. Do not publish private business or customer information.
- Make each project readable by someone without technical training.

## Networking

Networking means building professional relationships to learn and to be visible. It does not mean asking strangers for jobs.

- [ ] Identify professionals in roles I am exploring
- [ ] Prepare a short introduction based on my [Professional Statement](../About/Professional-Statement.md)
- [ ] Prepare questions about their work and how they entered the field
- [ ] Attend instructor office hours, student groups, or professional association events
- [ ] Follow up with a thank-you message after conversations
- [ ] Record contacts and insights in [Industry Research](Industry-Research.md)

## Career Readiness Checklist

- [ ] I can explain my direction in one or two sentences.
- [ ] My resume lists only verified information.
- [ ] My LinkedIn profile matches my resume.
- [ ] I have at least one documented project I can discuss.
- [ ] I can describe how I used a tool to answer a business question.
- [ ] I have researched roles and employers of interest.
- [ ] I have practiced describing my limitations honestly.
