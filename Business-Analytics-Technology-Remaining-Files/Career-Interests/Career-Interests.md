# Career Interests

## Purpose

Business analytics, data analytics, finance, accounting, marketing, and operations share many skills and tools. Early in my development, I want to understand those overlaps before narrowing my direction.

## How the Fields Overlap

| Shared element | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Spreadsheet analysis | Nearly every role |
| SQL and database querying | Data, business intelligence, business analyst, and operations roles |
| Dashboards and visualization | Business intelligence, performance, marketing, and finance roles |
| Financial literacy | Financial, accounting, performance, and e-commerce roles |
| Process thinking | Business systems, operations, and business analyst roles |
| Stakeholder communication | All roles |

## Roles at a Glance

| Role | Primary focus | Closest overlap |
|---|---|---|
| Business Analyst | Requirements and solutions for business problems | Business Systems Analyst, Operations Analyst |
| Data Analyst | Extracting and interpreting data | Business Intelligence Analyst |
| Business Intelligence Analyst | Reporting, dashboards, and data models | Data Analyst, Business Performance Analyst |
| Financial Analyst | Financial planning, forecasting, and evaluation | Accounting Analyst, Business Performance Analyst |
| Accounting Analyst | Accuracy and analysis of accounting data | Financial Analyst |
| Marketing Analyst | Campaign and customer performance | E-commerce Analyst, Data Analyst |
| Operations Analyst | Efficiency, capacity, and workflow | Business Analyst, Business Performance Analyst |
| Business Systems Analyst | Aligning business needs and technology systems | Business Analyst |
| E-commerce Analyst | Online sales, traffic, and conversion | Marketing Analyst, Data Analyst |
| Business Performance Analyst | KPIs and performance reporting | Financial Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst |

Details for each role are in [Roles to Explore](Roles-to-Explore.md).

## How I Will Evaluate Each Path

Questions I will ask as I study and practice:

- [ ] Which tasks do I enjoy most: building models, writing queries, designing reports, or explaining findings?
- [ ] Which types of business problems hold my attention?
- [ ] Which roles use the skills I most want to build?
- [ ] What do real job postings and professionals describe as typical work?
- [ ] Which role would allow me to apply my business interests?
