# Specialization Areas

## Purpose

This document describes analytics specializations I am considering and how I might apply them to business scenarios. These are areas of study and potential application. **They are not claims of completed projects.**

## Specialization Areas

| Area | Key questions | Common measures | Tools I am learning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial analytics | Is the business profitable? How is cash moving? | Gross margin, net margin, operating expenses, cash flow | Excel, SQL, Power BI |
| Marketing analytics | Which channels and campaigns produce results? | Conversion rate, cost per acquisition, return on ad spend | Excel, Power BI, web analytics tools |
| Sales analytics | What drives revenue, and where are the opportunities? | Revenue, average order value, sales growth, pipeline | Excel, SQL, Power BI |
| Operational analytics | Where are the delays, costs, and inefficiencies? | Cycle time, inventory turnover, throughput, error rate | Excel, SQL, process mapping |
| E-commerce analytics | How do visitors become customers, and which products perform best? | Traffic, conversion rate, cart behavior, repeat purchase rate | Excel, SQL, Power BI |
| Business intelligence | How should performance be monitored on an ongoing basis? | KPI sets, dashboards, report usage | Power BI, SQL, Excel |
| Process improvement | How can a workflow be made faster, cheaper, or more reliable? | Time saved, defects reduced, cost reduced | Process mapping, Excel |
| Business performance measurement | How well is the organization achieving its goals? | KPI frameworks, variance to plan | Excel, Power BI |

The table below lists analyses I could perform when I have suitable data. Each depends on having accurate records. I will document data sources and limitations for any real analysis, and I will protect customer and financial information.

| Application area | Example questions | Data I would need | Possible outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue and profitability analysis | Which products or services contribute most to profit? | Sales records, costs, fees | Margin analysis, profitability summary |
| Marketing performance | Which campaigns or channels bring in customers at a reasonable cost? | Campaign spend, traffic, conversions | Channel comparison, KPI dashboard |
| Customer behavior | Who buys, how often, and what do they buy? | Order history, customer segments | Repeat purchase analysis, segmentation |
| Sales performance | How do sales change over time and by product? | Transaction data | Trend charts, sales report |
| Business KPIs | Which measures best show whether the business is healthy? | Financial and operational records | KPI framework and scorecard |
| Financial reporting | What do the income statement and cash flow show? | Bookkeeping records | Monthly financial summary |
| Inventory and operations | Which items move quickly or slowly? Where are delays? | Inventory and fulfillment records | Turnover analysis, workflow map |
| E-commerce performance | Where do visitors drop off before purchase? | Store and web analytics | Funnel analysis, product dashboard |
| Business growth opportunities | Which segments or offers have the strongest potential? | Combined sales, marketing, and cost data | Opportunity analysis with recommendations |
