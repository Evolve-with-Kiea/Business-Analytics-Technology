# Business Analytics Technology

This repository documents my education, professional development, skills, career exploration, and learning roadmap as I build capabilities at the intersection of business and technology.

## Professional Direction

I am exploring how business knowledge and analytical tools work together to help organizations understand performance, solve problems, improve operations, and make informed decisions. Areas of interest include business analytics, data analytics, business intelligence, financial and accounting analysis, marketing analytics, e-commerce analytics, and process improvement.

This is a developing learning record. Skills, coursework, and credentials are described according to their actual status. Planned learning is not represented as completed experience.

## Learning Principles

1. Understand the business question before choosing a tool.
2. Learn concepts before relying on formulas, code, or software.
3. Practice with realistic, appropriately sourced data.
4. Document what was actually completed and what remains in progress.
5. Connect analysis to clear findings and useful recommendations.
6. Update this repository as coursework, skills, and career direction develop.
