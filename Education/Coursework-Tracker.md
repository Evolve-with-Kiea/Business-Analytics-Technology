# Coursework Tracker

## Purpose

This Coursework Tracker documents my academic progress, completed courses, developing technical skills, and practical learning experiences as I pursue an Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College.

Building upon more than 20 years of professional experience in accounting, financial operations, bookkeeping, reconciliations, and enterprise business systems, I am continuing my education to expand my knowledge of business analytics, data analysis, business intelligence, financial analysis, and technology-driven decision-making.

This document serves as a centralized academic record connecting formal coursework with practical learning, professional development, and future portfolio projects.

**Documentation Standard:** Course information, completion dates, grades, learning outcomes, and project details will be added only when confirmed through official academic records, course syllabi, or completed work.
