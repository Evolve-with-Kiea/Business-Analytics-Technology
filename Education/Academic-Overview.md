# Academic Overview

## Purpose & Academic Direction

My educational journey reflects a commitment to continuous learning, professional advancement, and the integration of business knowledge with modern analytical and technological skills.

With more than 20 years of experience in accounting, bookkeeping, financial operations, reconciliations, and enterprise business systems, I am continuing my education to expand into Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Financial Analysis, and related professional fields.

I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027.

Following completion of my associate degree, I intend to pursue a bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, or a closely related discipline.

My goal is to combine established financial and operational expertise with formal education, technical proficiency, and practical analytical experience.
