# Practice Log

## Purpose

This Practice Log documents my ongoing technical learning, analytical exercises, practical applications, and professional development as I expand my skills in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Financial Analysis, and related disciplines.

With more than 20 years of experience in accounting, financial operations, reconciliations, bookkeeping, and enterprise business systems, I am building upon my professional background through formal education and independent technical training.

I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027.

This log serves as a record of what I actually study, practice, understand, and apply. It also supports my [Learning Roadmap](README.md), [Technical Skills](../Skills/Technical-Skills.md), and [Skills Evidence](../Skills/Skills-Evidence.md).

**Documentation principle:** Entries are added only for learning sessions and exercises I have completed. Planned activities, sample entries, and future projects are not represented as completed work.

## My Learning Approach

My learning process follows a practical cycle:

**Learn → Understand → Practice → Apply → Reflect → Improve → Document**

I prioritize understanding concepts over memorizing solutions or rushing through lessons.

For each learning session, I aim to answer five questions:

1. What did I study?
2. Can I explain the concept in my own words?
3. What did I practice independently?
4. How could this concept apply to a business problem?
5. What do I need to review or learn next?

Not every session needs to produce a finished project. Understanding one difficult concept, correcting an error, or successfully completing an exercise independently represents meaningful progress.

## Technical Learning Notes

### Python Practice

My Python learning focuses on understanding programming fundamentals before advancing into analytical libraries or more complex projects.

Topics may include:

- Variables and data types
- Strings and numbers
- Operators
- Conditional statements
- Loops
- Functions
- Lists and dictionaries
- Error messages and debugging
- File handling
- Introductory data analysis

**Practice method:**

1. Complete a lesson.
2. Explain the concept in plain language.
3. Modify a working example.
4. Predict the output.
5. Run the code and compare the result.
6. Correct mistakes.
7. Complete a similar exercise independently.
8. Document what I learned.

Learning resources may include freeCodeCamp and other structured programming platforms.

### Excel Practice

Excel learning sessions may cover:

- Data organization and cleaning
- Formulas and functions
- Logical and lookup functions
- Excel Tables
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Power Query
- Charts and visualization
- KPI calculations
- Dashboard layout and interactivity

A major learning objective is developing the ability to turn raw financial or business data into meaningful reports and dashboards.

### SQL Practice

SQL learning sessions may cover:

- Relational database fundamentals
- SELECT statements
- WHERE and ORDER BY
- Aggregate functions
- GROUP BY and HAVING
- JOINs
- Subqueries
- Business reporting queries

Practice will emphasize answering specific business questions rather than memorizing SQL syntax without context.

### Power BI Practice

Power BI learning sessions may cover:

- Data import
- Power Query transformations
- Data modeling
- Relationships
- DAX measures
- Visualizations
- Report filters and slicers
- Interactive dashboards
- Business performance reporting

The objective is to develop reports that communicate relevant information clearly and support practical decisions.

## Documentation & Professional Standards

To maintain a credible learning record:

- I will record completed activities accurately.
- I will distinguish guided practice from independent work.
- I will identify external resources and data sources when appropriate.
- I will not present tutorials or copied solutions as original independent projects.
- I will document relevant mistakes, revisions, and limitations.
- I will avoid publishing confidential employer, customer, or academic information.
- I will share course assignments only when permitted.
- I will update my portfolio and skills records based on demonstrated progress.

The purpose of this log is not to create the appearance of constant productivity. It is to maintain an honest record of meaningful learning and improvement.
