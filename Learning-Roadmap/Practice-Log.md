# Practice Log

This log records what I study and practice. It supports accountability and provides evidence for [Skills Evidence](../Skills/Skills-Evidence.md). Entries are added as I complete sessions. I do not backfill entries for work I did not do.

## How to Log a Session

Record the date, the area, what I did, what I learned, and what remains unclear.
