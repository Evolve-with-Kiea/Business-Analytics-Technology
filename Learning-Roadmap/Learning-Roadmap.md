# Learning Roadmap

## My Journey from Accounting & Financial Operations to Business Analytics

### Purpose

This roadmap outlines my ongoing professional and technical development as I transition from more than 20 years of experience in accounting, financial operations, bookkeeping, reconciliations, and enterprise business systems into Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Financial Analysis, and related fields.

I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027. Following this degree, I intend to pursue a bachelor's degree in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, or a related discipline.

My goal is to build upon my existing business and financial knowledge while developing the technical skills needed to analyze data, create dashboards, improve business processes, evaluate performance, and communicate actionable recommendations.

This roadmap is organized into five flexible phases. It combines formal education, independent study, practical exercises, portfolio development, and professional preparation.

**My guiding principle: Learn → Practice → Apply → Analyze → Improve → Document → Demonstrate.**

---

## Learning Philosophy

My approach emphasizes understanding, practical application, and steady progress rather than rushing through courses or collecting credentials.

### Core Principles

**1. Understanding Before Speed**

I want to understand why a concept works, when to use it, and how it applies to a real business problem before moving to more advanced topics.

**2. Learn One Concept at a Time**

Complex subjects such as Python, SQL, Power BI, and analytical modeling will be broken into manageable lessons with examples, practice exercises, and opportunities to check my understanding.

**3. Build on Existing Experience**

My accounting and financial operations background provides a practical foundation for understanding revenue, expenses, cash flow, reconciliations, business processes, reporting, and performance measurement.

**4. Practice With Realistic Business Problems**

Whenever possible, I will work with sample datasets and scenarios involving finance, sales, marketing, e-commerce, and operations.

**5. Maintain Flexible Timelines**

Learning progress will depend on coursework, employment, personal responsibilities, and the complexity of each subject. Phases may overlap, and timelines can be adjusted without compromising the overall direction.

**6. Document What I Can Demonstrate**

I will distinguish between subjects I am studying, skills I am practicing, and capabilities I can demonstrate through completed work.

---

## Five-Phase Learning Framework

| Phase | Primary Focus | Core Tools & Subjects | Intended Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Business & Excel Foundations | Accounting, finance, business concepts, Excel | Understand business data and perform foundational analysis |
| Phase 2 | Programming & Analytical Practice | Python, Excel dashboards, analytical reasoning | Build basic programs and practical reporting solutions |
| Phase 3 | Databases & Business Intelligence | SQL, databases, Power BI, data modeling, DAX | Query data and develop interactive business reports |
| Phase 4 | Specialized Business Analytics | Financial, marketing, sales, operational, and e-commerce analytics | Apply analytical methods to specific business functions |
| Phase 5 | Professional Application | Portfolio projects, certifications, communication, career preparation | Demonstrate practical skills and professional readiness |

These phases represent learning priorities rather than rigid prerequisites. Some subjects will be studied simultaneously.

# Phase 1: Business & Excel Foundations

## Objective

Strengthen my ability to organize, analyze, interpret, and communicate business and financial information using Microsoft Excel and foundational analytical methods.

My previous accounting education and professional experience provide a foundation in business operations and financial reporting. This phase focuses on extending that knowledge into structured data analysis.

### Learning Priorities

- [ ] Review core business concepts: revenue, costs, profitability, margins, and operations.
- [ ] Review financial statements and key financial ratios.
- [ ] Strengthen Excel formulas and functions.
- [ ] Practice data organization and formatting.
- [ ] Learn data cleaning and validation techniques.
- [ ] Practice sorting, filtering, and Excel Tables.
- [ ] Develop proficiency with SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, IF, XLOOKUP, and related functions.
- [ ] Learn PivotTables and PivotCharts.
- [ ] Explore Power Query for data preparation.
- [ ] Practice creating effective charts and summaries.
- [ ] Write short interpretations of analytical findings.

### Practical Exercises

**Exercise 1: Financial Statement Analysis**

Create a sample income statement and calculate revenue growth, gross profit, operating expenses, and net profit margin.

**Exercise 2: Data Cleaning**

Clean a sample sales or accounting dataset by identifying duplicates, missing values, inconsistent formatting, and incorrect data types.

**Exercise 3: Accounts Receivable Analysis**

Analyze a fictional accounts receivable aging report and identify overdue balances, aging categories, and collection priorities.

**Exercise 4: Basic Business Performance Report**

Build a simple Excel report showing sales, expenses, profitability, and changes over time.

### Recommended Portfolio Project

**Accounts Receivable Aging & Collections Dashboard**

Potential components:

- Total outstanding receivables
- Current versus overdue balances
- Aging categories
- Customer balance comparisons
- Collection trends
- Key findings and suggested actions

**Status:** Proposed

### Phase 1 Milestone

I should be able to organize and clean business data, calculate meaningful financial metrics, create basic visualizations, and explain findings in plain language.

# Phase 2: Programming & Analytical Practice

## Objective

Develop foundational programming skills while strengthening analytical thinking and learning how to transform business data into useful information.

This phase introduces Python and expands Excel dashboard development.

### Python Fundamentals

- [ ] Understand variables and data types.
- [ ] Work with strings and numbers.
- [ ] Practice operators and expressions.
- [ ] Learn conditional statements.
- [ ] Understand loops and iteration.
- [ ] Practice lists, dictionaries, and other basic collections.
- [ ] Learn functions and reusable code.
- [ ] Read and interpret error messages.
- [ ] Practice debugging.
- [ ] Work with basic file input and output when ready.
- [ ] Explore pandas and data analysis libraries after mastering the fundamentals.

### Python Learning Method

For each new concept:

1. Read or watch the lesson.
2. Explain the concept in my own words.
3. Study a small working example.
4. Modify the example and observe the results.
5. Complete a similar exercise independently.
6. Identify and correct mistakes.
7. Connect the concept to a business scenario.
8. Document what I learned.

I am using beginner-friendly resources such as freeCodeCamp to strengthen my programming foundation.

### Excel Dashboard Development

- [ ] Identify relevant KPIs.
- [ ] Prepare and organize raw data.
- [ ] Use formulas to calculate performance measures.
- [ ] Build PivotTables.
- [ ] Create PivotCharts and visualizations.
- [ ] Explore slicers and interactive filters.
- [ ] Design clear dashboard layouts.
- [ ] Explain dashboard findings and limitations.

### Practical Exercises

**Exercise 1: Python Business Calculations**

Write small programs that calculate revenue, expenses, profit margins, or percentage changes.

**Exercise 2: Python Data Organization**

Use lists and dictionaries to organize simple business information.

**Exercise 3: Excel Sales Dashboard**

Develop a dashboard showing sales trends, product performance, revenue, and profitability using a fictional business dataset.

**Exercise 4: Business Case Study**

Analyze a business scenario, identify the central problem, select useful metrics, and write recommendations supported by the data.

### Recommended Portfolio Project

**Excel Sales & Profitability Dashboard**

# Phase 3: Databases & Business Intelligence

- [ ] Understand relational database concepts.
- [ ] Identify tables, rows, columns, and keys.
- [ ] Learn SELECT statements.
- [ ] Filter data using WHERE.
- [ ] Sort results using ORDER BY.
- [ ] Practice aggregate functions.
- [ ] Group data using GROUP BY.
- [ ] Filter grouped results using HAVING.
- [ ] Combine tables using JOINs.
- [ ] Explore subqueries and common table expressions.
- [ ] Practice writing queries that answer business questions.

### Power BI Learning Priorities

- [ ] Understand the Power BI interface.
- [ ] Import Excel and CSV datasets.
- [ ] Prepare data using Power Query.
- [ ] Understand tables and relationships.
- [ ] Develop basic data models.
- [ ] Learn calculated columns and measures.
- [ ] Practice introductory DAX.
- [ ] Create KPI cards, charts, and tables.
- [ ] Use slicers and report filters.
- [ ] Design interactive reports.
- [ ] Apply data visualization principles.
- [ ] Interpret results and communicate recommendations.

**Exercise 1: SQL Financial Reporting**

**Exercise 2: SQL Sales Analysis**

Use SQL to calculate sales by product, customer, category, or time period.

**Exercise 3: Power BI Data Model**

Create relationships between sample sales, products, customers, and calendar tables.

**Exercise 4: Power BI Performance Report**

Develop an interactive report showing financial or operational KPIs.

### Recommended Portfolio Project

**Power BI Executive Business Performance Dashboard**

Potential components:

- Financial KPIs

## How I Will Measure Learning Progress

Progress will be evaluated through practical evidence rather than course completion alone.

| Development Stage | Meaning | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Exploring | Becoming familiar with a subject | Introductory notes and examples |
| Learning | Studying foundational concepts | Lessons and guided exercises |
| Practicing | Applying concepts with assistance | Completed practice exercises |
| Developing | Solving increasingly independent problems | Small projects and case studies |
| Demonstrating | Completing work with clear explanations | Documented portfolio projects |
| Strengthening | Improving accuracy, efficiency, and depth | Revised projects and advanced applications |

I will avoid describing a skill as proficient solely because I completed an introductory course.

| Activity | Suggested Time |
|---|---|
