# Questions to Explore

## Business Analytics, Technology & Professional Development

### Purpose

This document serves as an evolving collection of questions that guide my education, independent research, technical practice, analytical reasoning, and professional development.

With more than 20 years of experience in accounting, financial operations, bookkeeping, reconciliations, and enterprise business systems, I am expanding my knowledge through formal education and practical training in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Financial Analysis, and related fields.

As I pursue my Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, I use these questions to connect established business knowledge with emerging technical skills in Microsoft Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, data visualization, business performance measurement, and analytical problem-solving.

**My objective is not simply to find answers. It is to understand how to investigate business problems, evaluate evidence, recognize limitations, and communicate useful conclusions.**

---

## How I Use This Resource

Questions are organized by subject and can be explored through coursework, tutorials, textbooks, practical exercises, research, or portfolio projects.

A question is considered explored when I can:

1. Explain the concept accurately in my own words.
2. Identify when and why the concept matters.
3. Demonstrate an example or practical application when appropriate.
4. Recognize important assumptions or limitations.
5. Document the answer and link supporting evidence.

### Question Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not Started | Question identified but not yet explored |
| Researching | Reading, studying, or reviewing relevant concepts |
| Practicing | Applying the concept through examples or exercises |
| Answered | Written explanation supported by appropriate evidence |
| Revisit | Additional practice or deeper understanding needed |

A completed checkbox indicates that I have documented an answer, not that I have mastered every related skill.
