# Certification Plan

## Purpose & Professional Development Strategy

This document outlines my certification interests, technical training priorities, and long-term professional development strategy as I transition from accounting and financial operations into Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and related analytical fields.

With more than 20 years of professional experience in accounting, bookkeeping, reconciliations, financial operations, and enterprise business systems, I am building upon an established business foundation rather than beginning an entirely new career.

I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027, and plan to continue toward a bachelor's degree in Business Analytics or a related discipline.

My certification strategy focuses on practical technical development, career relevance, affordability, and demonstrated skills.

**My objective is not to collect as many certifications as possible. It is to earn credentials that complement my education, strengthen my qualifications, and support practical, documented analytical work.**

## Certification Status & Terminology

This document includes credentials I have completed, credentials I am considering, and longer-term possibilities.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Earned | Credential completed and supported by provider documentation |
| In Progress | Currently studying or enrolled |
| Planned | Intend to pursue after meeting learning prerequisites |
| Exploring | Researching value, cost, requirements, and relevance |
| Deferred | Potential future credential, not currently prioritized |
| Not Pursuing | Evaluated and determined not to align with current goals |

Credential categories include courses, completion certificates, professional certificates, exam-based industry certifications, and professional licenses.

These categories are not interchangeable. Completing a course does not necessarily mean earning an independent professional certification.

Credential names, exams, fees, eligibility requirements, and renewal policies must be verified with the issuing provider before registration.

## Recommended Certification Sequence

This sequence is designed to support practical skill development without making every credential a requirement.

| Phase | Approximate Period | Primary Focus | Certification Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Late 2026 | Excel, Python fundamentals, introductory SQL | Continue free training; assess Excel certification readiness |
| Applied Learning | January–Summer 2027 | Business Analytics coursework, Excel dashboards, SQL, Power BI | Consider MOS Excel and prepare for PL-300 |
| Career Transition | Late 2027 | Portfolio development, advanced reporting, job applications | Consider PL-300 and ECBA based on readiness |
| Specialization | 2028 onward | Financial Analytics, BI, Business Systems, or Marketing Analytics | Select additional credentials based on chosen career direction |

**These are proposed planning periods, not guaranteed exam or completion dates.**

Academic requirements, practical projects, employment priorities, and business launch responsibilities take precedence over optional certification deadlines.

## Certification Decision Framework

Before registering for a program or exam, I will evaluate:

- [ ] Does this credential support a specific target role?
- [ ] Is it recognized or requested by relevant employers?
- [ ] Does it duplicate knowledge covered by my degree?
- [ ] Is the issuing provider reputable?
- [ ] What are the total costs, prerequisites, exam requirements, and renewal obligations?
- [ ] Can I access quality free or low-cost preparation resources?
- [ ] Have I demonstrated the underlying skills through practical work?
- [ ] Will the credential strengthen my portfolio and professional positioning?
- [ ] Does the timing align with my academic, career, and business responsibilities?

## Portfolio Integration

Certifications alone do not demonstrate the full ability to solve business problems.

For each major learning area, I aim to produce documented evidence that includes:

1. Business problem or objective
2. Data source and limitations
3. Tools and methods used
4. Analytical calculations and validation
5. Visualizations or reporting outputs
6. Findings and recommendations
7. Lessons learned and next improvements

This approach will allow employers and collaborators to review practical evidence of my developing analytical capabilities.
