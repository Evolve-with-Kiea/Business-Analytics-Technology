## How to Read This Plan

This is a planning document. **None of the items below have been earned.** They are options I am considering.

Program names, exam codes, prerequisites, and costs change. Before enrolling, I verify details on the provider's official site. The estimated cost column tells me where to check, and I replace it with the verified amount.

## Choosing Credentials

Questions I ask before enrolling:

- [ ] Does this credential match my current learning phase in the [Learning Roadmap](../Learning-Roadmap/Learning-Roadmap.md)?
- [ ] Is it a course, a completion certificate, a professional certificate, or an exam-based certification?
- [ ] Do the roles I am researching mention it?
- [ ] What are the prerequisites, cost, renewal terms, and exam format?
- [ ] Will I be able to apply the knowledge in a documented project afterward?
