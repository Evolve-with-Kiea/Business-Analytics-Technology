# Roles to Explore

## Purpose

This document summarizes ten roles I am exploring. It reflects general information about these positions. Actual duties, tools, and requirements vary by employer, industry, and seniority, so I verify details through job postings, professional associations, and sources such as the [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/) and [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org/).

The example projects are ideas that could demonstrate readiness. They are not completed work. When I complete one, I will add it to [Skills Evidence](../Skills/Skills-Evidence.md).

## Business Analyst

**What the position does:** Identifies business needs, analyzes processes and data, and recommends solutions that improve how an organization works.

**Common responsibilities**

- Gather and document requirements from stakeholders
- Map current and future processes
- Analyze data to support recommendations
- Write specifications, user stories, or reports
- Support testing and implementation of changes

**Technical skills required**

- Excel, basic SQL, data visualization, documentation and modeling methods

**Business knowledge required**

- Business processes, requirements analysis, stakeholder management, problem solving

**Common software and tools**

- Excel, SQL, Power BI, Jira, Microsoft Visio or similar diagramming tools, Microsoft Office

**Relevant certifications**

- IIBA ECBA, CCBA, CBAP; PMI CAPM; Agile-related credentials

**Potential career progression**

- Junior or associate business analyst, business analyst, senior business analyst, product owner, project or program manager

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Document a current-state and future-state process for a sample business
- Write requirements for a small reporting system
- Complete a case study with recommendations

## Data Analyst

**What the position does:** Collects, cleans, and analyzes data to answer questions and support decisions.

**Common responsibilities**

- Query databases
- Clean and prepare datasets
- Create reports and visualizations
- Identify trends and anomalies
- Communicate findings

**Technical skills required**

- SQL, Excel, Python, statistics fundamentals, data visualization

**Business knowledge required**

- Understanding of the business area, KPI definitions, data quality concerns

**Common software and tools**

- SQL, Excel, Python, Power BI or similar BI tools

**Relevant certifications**

- Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate, IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate, Power BI Data Analyst Associate, Python Institute certifications

**Potential career progression**

- Junior data analyst, data analyst, senior analyst, analytics manager, data scientist or analytics engineer (additional skills needed)

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Clean and analyze a public dataset and document the steps
- Answer five business questions using SQL
- Build a short report with findings and limitations

## Business Intelligence Analyst

**What the position does:** Designs reports, dashboards, and data models that help an organization monitor performance.

**Common responsibilities**

- Build and maintain dashboards
- Design data models
- Define metrics with stakeholders
- Validate data accuracy
- Train users to interpret reports

**Technical skills required**

- SQL, Power BI or similar tools, data modeling, DAX or equivalent calculation languages

**Business knowledge required**

- KPI design, reporting needs of different audiences, data governance basics

**Common software and tools**

- Power BI, SQL, Excel, data warehouse tools

**Relevant certifications**

- Microsoft Power BI Data Analyst Associate, Azure Data Fundamentals

**Potential career progression**

- BI analyst, senior BI analyst, BI developer, analytics manager, data architect (additional skills needed)

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Build a multi-page Power BI report from a public dataset
- Document a data model and its relationships
- Write a dashboard guide for non-technical users

## Financial Analyst

**What the position does:** Evaluates financial data to support budgeting, forecasting, and investment or operating decisions.

**Common responsibilities**

- Build and maintain financial models
- Prepare budgets and forecasts
- Analyze variances
- Report on financial performance
- Support management decisions

**Technical skills required**

- Advanced Excel, financial modeling, basic SQL, data visualization

**Business knowledge required**

- Accounting, financial statements, ratios, valuation basics, budgeting

**Common software and tools**

- Excel, ERP or accounting systems, Power BI, financial databases

**Relevant certifications**

- Financial modeling programs, CFA path (long-term), accounting coursework

**Potential career progression**

- Financial analyst, senior financial analyst, finance manager, FP&A manager, controller (with additional accounting experience)

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Build a budget versus actual variance analysis
- Create a three-statement model for a hypothetical company
- Analyze profitability by product line using sample data

## Accounting Analyst

**What the position does:** Reviews and analyzes accounting records to ensure accuracy and to support financial reporting.

**Common responsibilities**

- Reconcile accounts
- Prepare journal entries and schedules
- Analyze account balances
- Support month-end close
- Assist with audits

**Technical skills required**

- Excel, accounting software, basic SQL

**Business knowledge required**

- Accounting principles, financial statements, internal controls, GAAP basics

**Common software and tools**

- Excel, QuickBooks or ERP systems

**Relevant certifications**

- QuickBooks certification, CPA licensure (long-term and state-specific)

**Potential career progression**

- Accounting analyst, senior accountant, accounting manager, controller

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Prepare a sample reconciliation
- Build a depreciation schedule
- Explain how a set of transactions flows into financial statements

## Marketing Analyst

**What the position does:** Measures and interprets marketing performance to improve campaigns and customer engagement.

**Common responsibilities**

- Track campaign performance
- Analyze channels and audiences
- Report on conversion and spend
- Run and assess tests
- Recommend budget allocation

**Technical skills required**

- Excel, SQL, web analytics tools, data visualization, statistics fundamentals

**Business knowledge required**

- Marketing channels, customer journeys, attribution, pricing and positioning

**Common software and tools**

- Google Analytics, Excel, Power BI, HubSpot or other CRM and marketing platforms

**Relevant certifications**

- Google Analytics certification, HubSpot Academy certificates

**Potential career progression**

- Marketing analyst, senior marketing analyst, marketing analytics manager, growth or digital marketing manager

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Analyze a sample campaign dataset for return on spend
- Build a marketing KPI dashboard
- Write recommendations from a funnel analysis

## Operations Analyst

**What the position does:** Studies workflows, resources, and performance data to improve efficiency and reduce cost.

**Common responsibilities**

- Map and measure processes
- Identify bottlenecks and waste
- Forecast demand or capacity
- Track operational KPIs
- Recommend and evaluate improvements

**Technical skills required**

- Excel, SQL, process mapping tools, data visualization

**Business knowledge required**

- Operations management, supply chain basics, quality and process improvement

**Common software and tools**

- Excel, Power BI, ERP systems, process mapping software

**Relevant certifications**

- Lean Six Sigma credentials, PMI CAPM

**Potential career progression**

- Operations analyst, senior operations analyst, operations manager, process improvement manager

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Map a workflow and propose measurable improvements
- Analyze inventory turnover using sample data
- Create an operational KPI dashboard

## Business Systems Analyst

**What the position does:** Connects business needs with technology systems by analyzing requirements and supporting system design and implementation.

**Common responsibilities**

- Translate business needs into system requirements
- Work with technical teams
- Support testing and user training
- Document system processes
- Troubleshoot data and system issues

**Technical skills required**

- SQL, systems documentation, process modeling, familiarity with ERP and CRM concepts

**Business knowledge required**

- Business processes, systems integration concepts, stakeholder communication

**Common software and tools**

- SQL, Jira, diagramming tools, ERP and CRM systems

**Relevant certifications**

- IIBA certifications, PMI CAPM, vendor-specific system certifications

**Potential career progression**

- Business systems analyst, senior analyst, systems or solutions architect (additional skills needed), IT project manager

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Write system requirements for a sample business process
- Diagram data flow between business systems
- Prepare a test plan for a reporting change

## E-commerce Analyst

**What the position does:** Analyzes online sales, traffic, and customer behavior to improve digital store performance.

**Common responsibilities**

- Monitor sales, traffic, and conversion
- Analyze product and category performance
- Evaluate promotions and pricing
- Track inventory signals
- Report to marketing and operations teams

**Technical skills required**

- Excel, SQL, web analytics, data visualization

**Business knowledge required**

- Online retail metrics, customer behavior, merchandising, marketing channels

**Common software and tools**

- Google Analytics, e-commerce platform dashboards, Excel, Power BI

**Relevant certifications**

- Google Analytics certification, HubSpot Academy certificates

**Potential career progression**

- E-commerce analyst, senior analyst, e-commerce manager, digital strategy manager

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Analyze a sample store dataset for conversion and average order value
- Build a product performance dashboard
- Evaluate a hypothetical promotion

## Business Performance Analyst

**What the position does:** Tracks and interprets organizational KPIs and recommends actions to improve results.

**Common responsibilities**

- Define and maintain KPI frameworks
- Prepare performance reports
- Analyze variances and trends
- Support planning and strategy
- Present results to leaders

**Technical skills required**

- Excel, SQL, Power BI, data visualization

**Business knowledge required**

- KPI design, financial and operational metrics, strategy, reporting

**Common software and tools**

- Excel, Power BI, SQL, planning and ERP systems

**Relevant certifications**

- Power BI Data Analyst Associate, Lean Six Sigma credentials

**Potential career progression**

- Performance analyst, senior analyst, performance or strategy manager

**Example projects that demonstrate readiness**

- Design a KPI framework for a hypothetical business
- Build a balanced scorecard dashboard
- Write a performance review summary with recommendations
