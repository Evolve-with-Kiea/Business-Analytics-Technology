# Career Interests

## Purpose & Professional Direction

My career interests center on the intersection of accounting, finance, business operations, analytics, technology, and strategic decision-making.

With more than 20 years of experience in accounting and financial operations, I am expanding my professional capabilities through formal education in Business Analytics, independent technical learning, and applied projects.

My goal is to transition from primarily processing, reconciling, and maintaining financial information toward analyzing business performance, developing reporting solutions, evaluating operational challenges, and supporting informed business decisions.

I am particularly interested in roles that combine practical business knowledge with technical skills in Microsoft Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, and business intelligence.

Rather than choosing a career solely based on a job title, I want to understand the responsibilities, required skills, advancement opportunities, and practical business applications associated with each specialization.

## Career Roles at a Glance

### Business Analyst

**Primary focus:** Understanding business problems, identifying requirements, evaluating processes, and recommending improvements.

Typical activities include documenting workflows, gathering stakeholder requirements, identifying inefficiencies, evaluating solutions, and supporting business change.

**Why it interests me:** My accounting and financial operations experience provides familiarity with business processes, enterprise software, financial controls, and operational challenges.

### Data Analyst

**Primary focus:** Preparing, examining, and interpreting data to answer business questions.

Typical activities include data cleaning, SQL querying, exploratory analysis, KPI calculation, visualization, and communicating findings.

**Why it interests me:** I want to strengthen my ability to move beyond transactional data processing and use information to explain business trends and opportunities.

### Business Intelligence Analyst

**Primary focus:** Developing reporting systems, dashboards, data models, and performance measurement solutions.

Typical activities include building Power BI reports, defining KPIs, working with structured data, maintaining dashboards, and supporting management reporting.

**Why it interests me:** I am particularly interested in creating interactive dashboards that transform financial, marketing, and operational information into accessible business insights.

### Financial Analyst / Financial Data Analyst

**Primary focus:** Evaluating financial performance, budgets, forecasts, profitability, and business results.

Typical activities include financial modeling, variance analysis, forecasting, management reporting, and financial performance evaluation.

**Why it interests me:** This specialization offers a natural connection to my accounting background while expanding into more forward-looking financial analysis.

### Accounting Analyst

**Primary focus:** Analyzing accounting information, reconciliations, transactions, controls, and reporting accuracy.

**Why it interests me:** It provides a potential bridge between my established accounting experience and developing analytical capabilities.

### Reporting Analyst

**Primary focus:** Producing accurate, consistent, and useful business reports.

**Why it interests me:** Reporting roles can provide opportunities to apply Excel, SQL, and business intelligence tools while building on my financial operations experience.

### Business Systems Analyst

**Primary focus:** Connecting business requirements, enterprise applications, data, and technology solutions.

**Why it interests me:** My experience with QuickBooks, SAP, Ariba, Microsoft Dynamics, and accounting workflows provides useful context for understanding business systems and how they support operations.

### Operations Analyst

**Primary focus:** Evaluating operational efficiency, process performance, capacity, and workflow improvements.

**Why it interests me:** I have practical experience with transaction processing, reconciliations, discrepancies, and financial operations that can inform process analysis.

### Marketing Analyst

**Primary focus:** Evaluating marketing campaigns, customer acquisition, engagement, conversions, and return on investment.

**Why it interests me:** Marketing analytics supports my interests in entrepreneurship, business strategy, and the future development of Brand Evolution Marketing Agency.

### E-commerce Analyst

**Primary focus:** Understanding online sales, product performance, customer behavior, traffic, and conversion trends.

**Why it interests me:** This specialization has practical applications to Shop the Evolution™, including product performance, customer acquisition, profitability, and business growth.

For more detailed comparisons, see [Roles to Explore](Roles-to-Explore.md).
