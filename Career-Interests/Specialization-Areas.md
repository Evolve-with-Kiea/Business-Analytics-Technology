# Specialization Areas

## Purpose & Professional Direction

This document outlines the areas of business analytics, data analytics, and business intelligence I am exploring as I expand my professional experience into more analytical and technology-focused responsibilities.

With more than 20 years of accounting and financial operations experience, I bring an established understanding of financial transactions, reconciliations, enterprise accounting systems, business workflows, and operational accuracy.

I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027, while developing practical skills in Microsoft Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, data visualization, and analytical problem-solving.

My goal is to combine my existing business knowledge with developing technical capabilities to analyze performance, improve processes, create reporting solutions, and support informed decisions.

These specializations represent areas of study, professional interest, and potential business application. **They are not claims of completed projects, demonstrated technical proficiency, or currently available consulting services.**

## Primary Specialization Priorities

### 1. Financial & Operational Analytics

**Priority: High — Strongest connection to my professional experience**

This area builds directly on my background in accounting, accounts receivable, accounts payable, bookkeeping, cash applications, reconciliations, and financial operations.

I am interested in analyzing how financial information and operational processes affect business performance.

**Areas of focus:**
- Revenue, expenses, profitability, and cash flow
- Accounts receivable aging and collections performance
- Accounts payable and vendor payment analysis
- Budgeting, forecasting, and variance analysis
- Financial reporting and management dashboards
- Operational efficiency and process improvement

**Potential portfolio projects:**
- Accounts Receivable Aging & Collections Dashboard
- Budget vs. Actual Financial Performance Report
- Cash Flow and Working Capital Analysis
- Accounts Payable Processing Efficiency Analysis

### 2. Business Intelligence & Performance Reporting

**Priority: High — Primary technical development direction**

Business intelligence interests me because it combines analytical thinking, data preparation, reporting, visualization, and business decision-making.

My goal is to learn how to transform raw business data into structured, reliable, and understandable reports that help organizations monitor performance.

**Areas of focus:**
- KPI definition and calculation
- Dashboard design and interactive reporting
- Power BI and Excel reporting
- SQL querying and data preparation
- Data modeling and reporting accuracy
- Executive reporting and business performance measurement

**Potential portfolio projects:**
- Executive Business Performance Dashboard
- Multi-Department KPI Scorecard
- Power BI Sales and Profitability Report
- SQL-to-Dashboard Reporting Case Study

### 3. Marketing, Sales & E-commerce Analytics

**Priority: Developing — Strong connection to entrepreneurial goals**

I am interested in understanding how businesses attract customers, generate sales, measure marketing effectiveness, and improve customer experiences.

This specialization also supports my long-term interests in Shop the Evolution™ and Brand Evolution Marketing Agency.

**Areas of focus:**
- Marketing campaign performance
- Customer acquisition and conversion
- Sales trends and product performance
- Customer retention and repeat purchases
- Marketing return on investment
- E-commerce funnels and customer behavior

**Potential portfolio projects:**
- Marketing Campaign Performance Dashboard

- Product Category Profitability Report
- Customer Retention and Repeat Purchase Analysis

## Specialization Comparison

| Specialization | Key Business Questions | Example KPIs | Technical Tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial Analytics | Is the business profitable? How effectively is cash managed? | Gross margin, net margin, cash flow, budget variance | Excel, Power BI, SQL |
| Accounts Receivable Analytics | Which balances are overdue? Where are collection risks increasing? | Aging buckets, DSO, overdue balance %, collection rate | Excel, Power BI, SQL |
| Business Intelligence | How can performance be monitored consistently? | Revenue growth, KPI attainment, reporting accuracy | Power BI, SQL, Excel |
| Sales Analytics | Which products, customers, and periods drive revenue? | Revenue, sales growth, average order value | Excel, SQL, Power BI |
| Marketing Analytics | Which channels and campaigns generate valuable results? | Conversion rate, CPA, ROAS, marketing ROI | Google Analytics, Excel, Power BI |
| E-commerce Analytics | How do visitors become customers? Which products perform best? | Conversion rate, AOV, repeat purchase rate | E-commerce reporting, GA4, Excel, Power BI |
| Operational Analytics | Where do delays, costs, and inefficiencies occur? | Cycle time, throughput, error rate, cost per transaction | Excel, SQL, Power BI |
| Business Process Improvement | How can workflows become more efficient and reliable? | Time saved, error reduction, process cost | Process mapping, Excel |
| Business Performance Measurement | Is the organization meeting its goals? | KPI attainment, variance to target, performance trends | Excel, Power BI |
| Business Systems Analytics | How effectively do business systems support operations? | Data accuracy, processing errors, system-related delays | SQL, ERP systems, process mapping |

Tools listed represent learning priorities and potential applications rather than proficiency claims.
