# Resume and LinkedIn Notes

## Professional Branding, Career Positioning & Job Application Strategy

### Purpose

This document serves as a professional branding and career communication resource for developing, reviewing, and maintaining my resume, LinkedIn profile, and related professional materials.

My career background includes more than 20 years of experience in accounting, financial operations, accounts receivable, accounts payable, bookkeeping, reconciliations, and enterprise business systems.

I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027, while expanding my technical knowledge in Microsoft Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, and related analytical disciplines.

My professional objective is to communicate the connection between my established financial and operational expertise and my developing analytical capabilities.

**Professional positioning principle:** Present my experience, education, skills, and career direction accurately while demonstrating the value of combining business knowledge, analytical thinking, and technology.

This document contains drafting guidance and proposed language. All employment dates, accomplishments, degrees, credentials, technical skills, and measurable results must be verified before inclusion in a published resume or professional profile.

## 2. Accuracy & Documentation Standards

All professional materials should reflect verifiable qualifications.

### Resume and Profile Accuracy Rules

- Include only education, employment, certifications, and accomplishments supported by actual records.
- Describe technical skills according to demonstrated proficiency.
- Distinguish professional experience from academic learning.
- Avoid describing a tool as an established strength when I am still learning it.
- Use measurable accomplishments only when the figures can be verified.
- Avoid inventing efficiency improvements, cost savings, or performance percentages.
- Do not list proposed portfolio projects as completed work.
- Identify credentials as earned only after successful completion.
- Keep resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, and other professional materials consistent.
- Avoid sharing confidential employer or customer information.

### Appropriate Technical Skill Language

| Current Skill Level | Suggested Wording |
|---|---|
| Exploring | Exploring foundational concepts in... |
| Learning | Currently learning... |
| Practicing | Developing practical skills in... |
| Developing | Applying knowledge through exercises and projects in... |
| Demonstrating | Demonstrated experience through documented projects in... |
| Established Professional Experience | Professional experience using... |

For formal resumes, learning-stage wording should be used selectively and should not replace stronger evidence from established professional experience.

## 3. Resume Structure & Planning

### Recommended Resume Sections

| Section | Purpose | Documentation Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Contact Information | Provide professional contact channels | Verify links and details |
| Professional Summary | Communicate experience and career direction | Match verified background |
| Core Competencies | Highlight relevant business and technical capabilities | Use supportable skills |
| Professional Experience | Demonstrate responsibilities and accomplishments | Verify roles, employers, dates |
| Education | Present completed and ongoing education | Verify institution and status |
| Certifications | List earned professional credentials | Verify completion and dates |
| Projects | Demonstrate applied analytical skills | Include completed work only |
| Technical Tools | Identify software experience | Distinguish professional use from learning |

### Resume Formatting Principles

- Use a clean, professional layout.
- Prioritize readability and relevant qualifications.
- Use standard section headings.
- Avoid unnecessary graphics, tables, or decorative elements in ATS-focused resumes.
- Use consistent formatting and date styles.
- Tailor content to the position without changing factual information.
- Prioritize relevant experience over unrelated details.
- Save and review a final copy before submitting applications.

## 6. Professional Experience & Transferable Skills

### Connecting Accounting Experience to Analytics

Accounting and financial operations experience can provide valuable preparation for analytical work.

| Accounting / Operations Experience | Related Analytical Capability |
|---|---|
| Reconciliations | Data validation and discrepancy investigation |
| Accounts Receivable | Aging analysis and collections performance |
| Accounts Payable | Expense tracking and payment process analysis |
| Cash Applications | Transaction matching and financial data accuracy |
| Financial Reporting | KPI interpretation and management reporting |
| Bookkeeping | Financial data organization and business context |
| ERP Systems | Business systems understanding and data workflows |
| Process Coordination | Workflow analysis and operational improvement |
| Problem Resolution | Root cause investigation and analytical reasoning |

These connections describe transferable capabilities. They do not automatically establish proficiency in specialized analytics software.

### Experience Bullet Development

Use the following structure:

**Action Verb + Responsibility or Problem + Tool or Method + Verified Result or Business Purpose**

#### Sample Bullet Frameworks

- Managed [specific accounting process] using [verified software or method] to support accurate financial records and timely processing.
- Performed reconciliations involving [verified account or transaction type], investigating discrepancies and supporting financial accuracy.
- Coordinated [verified workflow] across [relevant departments or stakeholders] to support operational requirements.
- Prepared or maintained [verified reports or records] used for [documented business purpose].
- Identified and resolved [verified issue] through review of [relevant financial information or processes].

Replace placeholders with actual experience and avoid adding unverified metrics.

### Quantifying Accomplishments

Potential measurable details include:

- Number of accounts managed
- Transaction volume
- Number of entities supported
- Reporting frequency
- Reconciliation scope
- Processing turnaround times
- Verified error reductions
- Documented process improvements

Numbers should be included only when supported by records or reliable professional documentation.
