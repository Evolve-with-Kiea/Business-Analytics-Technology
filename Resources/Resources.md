# Business Analytics & Professional Learning Resources

## Technical Learning, Financial Analysis, Business Intelligence & Career Development

### Purpose

This resource directory organizes educational platforms, official documentation, professional organizations, public datasets, certification providers, and career research tools that support my continuing development in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Financial Analysis, Business Intelligence, Accounting, and related disciplines.

My professional background includes more than 20 years of experience in accounting and financial operations. I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027.

As I build upon my established financial and business experience, these resources support my development in Microsoft Excel, Python, SQL, Power BI, financial modeling, business reporting, data visualization, statistics, and business process improvement.

**Learning objective:** Use reliable educational resources to develop practical technical capabilities, strengthen analytical reasoning, and connect new concepts to meaningful business applications.

This directory is a curated reference, not a record of completed courses or verified certifications. Resource availability, pricing, access requirements, and course content may change.

---

## 1. Resource Selection & Learning Standards

### Resource Evaluation Criteria

Before relying on a resource, I will consider:

- **Credibility:** Is the information provided by an official organization, recognized educator, or reliable source?
- **Relevance:** Does the content support a current learning objective or career goal?
- **Practical Application:** Does the resource provide exercises, examples, datasets, or opportunities to practice?
- **Accessibility:** Is the resource available online and compatible with my schedule?
- **Cost:** Is the content free, included with existing access, or paid?
- **Currency:** Has the material been updated for relevant software versions or industry changes?
- **Learning Level:** Is it appropriate for my current understanding?
- **Documentation:** Can I record what I learned and demonstrate the skills developed?

### Preferred Learning Approach

My learning process follows:

**Learn → Understand → Practice → Apply → Analyze → Improve → Document**

I prioritize foundational understanding before moving into more advanced concepts.

Activities actually completed will be documented in the [Practice Log](../Learning-Roadmap/Practice-Log.md).

### Certification Evaluation Questions

Before pursuing a credential:

1. Is it relevant to my target roles?
2. Is it recognized by employers?
3. Does it require practical technical knowledge?
4. What is the total preparation and examination cost?
5. How much study time is realistic?
6. Does it complement my academic coursework?
7. Can I demonstrate the related skills through projects?
8. Is another credential more relevant to my current career stage?
