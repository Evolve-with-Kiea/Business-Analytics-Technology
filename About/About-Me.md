## My Approach to Learning & Professional Growth

I believe effective learning requires more than completing a course or memorizing formulas.

My approach follows a practical, repeatable process:

**Learn → Build → Measure → Analyze → Improve → Document → Teach → Apply**

1. **Understand the concept.** Learn what a formula, metric, analytical method, or technology means before applying it.
2. **Identify the business problem.** Understand what question needs to be answered and why the result matters.
3. **Practice with realistic scenarios.** Use sample datasets, guided exercises, and practical business problems.
4. **Validate the results.** Check calculations, investigate errors, question assumptions, and evaluate data quality.
5. **Interpret the findings.** Explain what the results reveal and how they could influence decisions.
6. **Document the process.** Record methods, examples, limitations, lessons learned, and completed work.
7. **Apply and improve.** Use feedback and additional practice to strengthen both technical and analytical skills.

I value accuracy, clarity, consistency, critical thinking, and continuous improvement.
