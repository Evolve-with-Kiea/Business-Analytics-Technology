This document defines my professional identity, career direction, and evolving value proposition as I build upon more than 20 years of accounting and business operations experience through formal education in Business Analytics and continued technical development.

## Core Professional Statement

I am an accounting and financial operations professional with more than 20 years of experience in accounts receivable, accounts payable, bookkeeping, financial reconciliations, business processes, and enterprise accounting systems. My background includes working with QuickBooks, SAP, Ariba, Microsoft Dynamics, and other financial management platforms, providing a practical understanding of financial information, operational workflows, accuracy, and business performance.

I am currently pursuing an Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics at Wake Technical Community College, with anticipated completion in Summer 2027, and plan to continue my education toward a bachelor's degree in Business Analytics or a related discipline.

My professional development focuses on combining established financial and operational knowledge with analytical thinking, data visualization, and technical capabilities in Microsoft Excel, Power BI, SQL, and Python.

My career objective is to transition into analytics-focused opportunities where I can evaluate business performance, investigate operational challenges, develop meaningful reporting solutions, and translate data into actionable business insights.

As an entrepreneur, I am also interested in applying these capabilities to e-commerce, marketing performance, financial analysis, and the future development of business intelligence tools and educational resources.

**My professional value lies in connecting real-world business experience with analytical methods and technology to support informed decisions, operational improvements, and sustainable business growth.**

## Professional Principles

My professional approach is guided by the following principles:

1. **Business understanding comes first.** Technology should support a clearly defined business question, problem, or objective.
2. **Accuracy and integrity matter.** Financial and analytical information must be reliable, properly validated, and responsibly presented.
3. **Experience and education complement each other.** Practical knowledge provides context, while new technical skills expand the methods available to solve problems.
4. **Analysis should lead to understanding.** Calculating metrics is only part of the process; interpreting results and explaining their implications creates value.
5. **Communication is essential.** Findings should be clear, relevant, and understandable to technical and non-technical audiences.
6. **Continuous learning is a professional responsibility.** As business needs and technologies evolve, skills and methods must evolve with them.
7. **Demonstrated work supports credibility.** Skills, certifications, projects, and accomplishments should be supported by verifiable evidence.
