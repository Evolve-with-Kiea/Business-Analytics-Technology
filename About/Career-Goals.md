# Career Goals

## Professional Vision

My career goal is to combine more than 20 years of accounting, financial operations, and business experience with advanced analytical and technical skills to pursue opportunities in Business Analytics, Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Financial Analysis, and Business Systems.

I am not beginning my professional career from scratch. I am building upon extensive experience in accounting, accounts receivable, accounts payable, bookkeeping, financial processes, reconciliations, and enterprise business systems.

Through formal education, independent learning, professional certifications, and applied projects, I intend to expand my ability to analyze business information, identify trends, evaluate performance, improve processes, and develop solutions that support better business decisions.

My long-term vision also includes applying these capabilities to my entrepreneurial ventures, developing analytical resources, and eventually providing business intelligence, reporting, and analytics solutions.

**My guiding objective is to move beyond processing and maintaining business information toward analyzing, interpreting, and using that information to influence business strategy and performance.**

---

## Career Exploration Approach

I am exploring several related career paths rather than committing prematurely to a single title.

My primary areas of interest are:

- **Business Analytics:** Evaluating business problems, performance, processes, and opportunities for improvement.
- **Data Analytics:** Collecting, cleaning, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting business data.
- **Business Intelligence:** Developing dashboards, reporting systems, performance metrics, and decision-support tools.
- **Financial Analytics:** Analyzing revenue, expenses, profitability, cash flow, and financial performance.
- **Business Systems Analysis:** Understanding how software, information systems, workflows, and business requirements work together.
- **Marketing and E-commerce Analytics:** Evaluating campaign performance, customer behavior, conversions, and business growth.
- **Operational Analytics:** Measuring efficiency, identifying bottlenecks, and improving business processes.

I will use coursework, technical exercises, portfolio projects, industry research, and professional opportunities to determine which specializations best align with my strengths and interests.

See [Roles to Explore](../Career-Interests/Roles-to-Explore.md).

1. **Build on my existing experience.** Apply my accounting, finance, and business operations background to more analytical and strategic responsibilities.
2. **Complete my Business Analytics education.** Earn my Associate of Applied Science in Business Analytics and continue toward a bachelor's degree in a related field.
3. **Develop technical proficiency.** Build practical skills in Excel, Power BI, SQL, Python, data preparation, reporting, and analytical problem-solving.
4. **Understand the complete analytical process.** Learn to define business questions, prepare data, calculate metrics, validate results, communicate findings, and recommend actions.
5. **Build a credible professional portfolio.** Demonstrate my abilities through documented projects, realistic datasets, dashboards, business case studies, and analytical reports.
6. **Earn relevant professional certifications.** Pursue credentials that support practical competence and career opportunities.
7. **Transition into analytics-focused work.** Seek opportunities that combine my established professional experience with developing analytical skills.
8. **Apply analytics to entrepreneurship.** Use business intelligence and performance measurement to support my own businesses and future client services.
9. **Continue learning as technology evolves.** Develop adaptability in analytics, automation, artificial intelligence, and emerging business technologies.
