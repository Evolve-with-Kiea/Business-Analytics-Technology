# Business and Analytical Skills

Technical tools are only useful when paired with business understanding and sound analytical thinking. This document describes the non-technical foundation I am building.

## Business Knowledge

| Area | What I need to understand | How I plan to develop it |
|---|---|---|
| Accounting and financial reporting | The income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement, and how transactions flow into them | Coursework, accounting resources, and worked examples |
| Financial analysis | Ratios, margins, profitability, and cash flow | Practice calculations with documented interpretation |
| Marketing | Customer acquisition, conversion, and campaign performance | Marketing analytics courses and practice with sample data |
| Sales and e-commerce | Revenue drivers, pricing, order value, and customer behavior | KPI study and scenario analysis |
| Operations | Workflows, capacity, inventory, and efficiency | Process mapping and operational case studies |
| Business strategy | How organizations set goals and measure progress | Coursework and industry research |
| Business technology | How systems and data support business processes | Coursework and tool exploration |

## Analytical Skills

| Skill | Description | How I plan to show it |
|---|---|---|
| Problem framing | Turning a vague concern into a specific, answerable question | State the business question at the top of every analysis |
| Data preparation | Cleaning and structuring data so results are reliable | Document cleaning steps and decisions |
| KPI selection | Choosing measures that reflect the goal | Explain why each KPI was chosen |
| Interpretation | Explaining what a result means in business terms | Write interpretation alongside every calculation |
| Critical thinking | Questioning assumptions, data quality, and causation | List limitations in every analysis |
| Data visualization | Selecting visuals that communicate accurately | Justify chart choices |
| Communication | Presenting findings and recommendations clearly | Write short summaries for non-technical readers |
| Process improvement thinking | Finding root causes of inefficiency | Document workflows and proposed changes |

## Analysis Standards I Follow

1. State the business question and the decision it supports.
2. Describe the data source and its limitations.
3. Show the calculation method, not only the result.
4. Interpret the result in plain language.
5. Note assumptions and what the analysis cannot tell me.
